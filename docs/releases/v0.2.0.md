Cropper 0.2.0 makes cropping easier to start and easier to review.

- Drag anywhere on a photo toward the center to resize the nearest crop corner. Shift-drag inside the crop to reposition it.
- See live original and cropped dimensions, the final cropped megapixel count, and the megapixel and percentage reduction.
- Review crops before saving with cropped-only, side-by-side, or top-and-bottom previews. Preferences can enable automatic preview after dragging.
- Press Enter to save or ⌘P to switch between preview and editing. + and − adjust retained image area by a configurable percentage-point step.
- A new native app icon appears in Finder and the Dock.

Download **Cropper-0.2.0-universal.zip**, unzip it, and drag **Cropper.app** into Applications. Requires macOS 14 or newer; supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. For first-launch instructions, see the [README](https://github.com/ThatXliner/Cropper#install). Originals are preserved by default. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the archive checksum.
